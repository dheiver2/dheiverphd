Frequently asked questions

How is this different from a technical audit?

Technical audits test the model. We assess the system around it: where humans stopped questioning outputs, where accountability is ambiguous, and where a plausible-but-wrong answer causes real damage. That is where most AI incidents actually start.

Do you need access to our code or data?

No. The assessment works through interviews, process review, and documentation analysis. That also means it can start immediately, without security clearances or NDAs over source code.

How long does an assessment take?

Typically a few weeks from kickoff to final presentation, depending on the number of AI use cases and teams involved.
