Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

This website is operated by Dheiver Santos, PhD ("we"). It respects your privacy and collects the minimum data necessary.

What we collect

  • Newsletter: if you subscribe, your email address is transmitted via FormSubmit and delivered to our inbox. It is used only to send you AI-related insights. No other personal data is requested.
  • Analytics: we use privacy-friendly, cookieless page analytics (Vercel Analytics) that do not identify you individually.
  • Contact: if you email us or message us on WhatsApp, we use your contact details only to reply.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing. We do not use advertising trackers or cross-site cookies.

Your rights (LGPD / GDPR)

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time — including unsubscribing from the newsletter — by writing to dheiver.santos@mangaba.ia.br.

Hosting

This site is hosted on Vercel. Server logs (IP address, user agent) may be processed by Vercel for security and operation of the service.
