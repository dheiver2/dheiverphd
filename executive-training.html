Frequently asked questions

Is this technical training?

No coding is involved. It is decision-level training: what AI failure modes look like from the leadership chair, and how they translate into financial, legal, and reputational exposure.

Who should attend?

Boards, C-suite, and senior functional leaders — anyone who approves, budgets, or depends on AI initiatives. Mixed groups work well because delegation risk lives between functions.

Can it be delivered remotely?

Yes. Programs run in-person or remote, in Portuguese, English, or Spanish, in single-session or multi-week formats.
